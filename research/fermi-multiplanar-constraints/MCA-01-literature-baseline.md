## Findings and tensions
1. A literature precedent for paradox attenuation exists; novelty cannot be claimed for that thesis alone.
2. The abiogenesis rate remains weakly constrained by Earth's single observation. Spiegel–Turner emphasize prior sensitivity; Kipping uses a different Bayesian construction and reaches more informative but still model-dependent conclusions. These are not interchangeable probability estimates.
3. Galactic habitability studies constrain environmental opportunity, not the full pathway to detectable intelligence.
4. Universal fine-tuning and local environmental filters must be modeled at different hierarchical levels.
