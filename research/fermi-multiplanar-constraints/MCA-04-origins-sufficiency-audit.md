# MCA-04: Explanatory Sufficiency, Origins Gate, and Methodological Symmetry Audit

**Programme:** Christian Designism / WP-MCA-0001  
**Status:** PROPOSED analytical protocol, not a completed adjudication  
**Date:** 2026-11-22  
**Purpose:** Correct a conflation in MCA-03 between quantitative underdetermination of extraterrestrial prevalence and comparative explanatory adequacy of biological-origin accounts.

## 1. Core correction

The statement "the frequency of extraterrestrial technological intelligence is underdetermined" concerns estimation of prevalence. It does **not** imply that every proposed origin-of-life mechanism is equally well supported, that causal gaps are irrelevant, or that all origin accounts have equal explanatory standing.

For naturally originating **biological** extraterrestrial intelligence, the emergence of a viable heritable living system is a necessary ancestral event. Any model that projects biological technological life must condition on that event. Its probability cannot be silently set to 1, inferred from the number of planets, or treated as solved because later Darwinian evolution is established.

This is a conditional claim about biological life. It does not by itself address nonbiological intelligence, engineered seeding, or whether an original biological lineage was produced by design.

## 2. Distinct research questions

- **Q1: Prevalence.** How many biological technological civilizations should exist or be detectable, given an explicit probability model?
- **Q2: Mechanism.** What empirically supported pathways can generate an evolvable biological system from antecedent nonliving chemistry?
- **Q3: Comparative explanation.** How do specified naturalistic and design-based hypotheses account for the evidence, including initial conditions, functional organization, information processing and causal history?
- **Q4: Inference limits.** What cannot be concluded without identified likelihoods, prior assumptions, and discriminating predictions?

A failure to calibrate Q1 cannot substitute for an evaluation of Q2 or Q3.

## 3. Origins gate: minimum requirements and careful typing

The research target is a **viable, heritable, evolving biological system**. Whether all modern-cell functions had to appear simultaneously is an empirical question. Candidate early systems may have differed radically from extant cells.

| Function or transition | Research obligation | Caution against overstatement |
|---|---|---|
| Accessible feedstocks and reaction environments | Show plausible chemical availability and reaction conditions | Laboratory feasibility is not a demonstrated historical pathway |
| Sustained energy coupling and reaction networks | Explain thermodynamic driving, resource cycling and persistence | Equilibrium arguments alone do not determine non-equilibrium pathways |
| Heritable information | Explain a mechanism of templating, copying or inheritance with variation | Modern DNA/RNA/protein coding architecture need not be present at the first evolutionary stage |
| Functional selection | Specify how functional variants arise and differential persistence operates | Natural selection presupposes a population with differential inheritance; prebiotic selection mechanisms require separate justification |
| Compartmentalization / system integrity | Show viable coupling among components and environmental exchange | Compartmentalization can be graded and pathway-dependent |
| Translation and coded protein synthesis | Explain the emergence of extant coding/decoding architecture along a proposed evolutionary path | Do not assume the modern genetic code was a prerequisite for the first replicator |
| Integrated complexity | Identify feasible intermediate states, not only endpoints | The probability of one modern sequence under uniform random assembly is not the probability of all plausible pathways |

**Gate judgment categories:** experimentally demonstrated step; experimentally supported partial mechanism; theoretically specified but unvalidated mechanism; conjectural bridge; contradicted under specified conditions; unknown. No category alone establishes a complete abiogenesis pathway.

## 4. Methodological-naturalism audit

Distinguish:
1. **Methodological naturalism:** a disciplinary convention restricting scientific explanations to natural causes.
2. **Metaphysical naturalism:** the philosophical thesis that only natural reality exists.
3. **Mechanistic natural-origin hypothesis:** a specific, falsifiable proposal about physical and chemical transitions.
4. **Design hypothesis:** a specified proposal that purposive agency contributed causally, with stated scope, mechanism or intervention class, and predicted observations.

A methodological restriction may delimit what a scientific model considers. It is not independent empirical confirmation of metaphysical naturalism. Conversely, absence of a complete natural-origin account does not by itself establish a particular design hypothesis.

### Bias audit questions

- Does a natural-origin proposal receive evidentiary credit for a merely possible but unverified transition?
- Is a design proposal rejected solely by an a priori exclusion of agency, without examining its empirical claims?
- Are unknown probabilities silently assigned favorable values in either model?
- Are naturalistic hypotheses allowed indefinite unspecified auxiliary mechanisms while design hypotheses face stricter specification demands?
- Are both accounts required to predict observations that differ under their respective models?
- Are we comparing actual specified hypotheses, or vague worldview labels?
- Are we treating the word "information" consistently (sequence entropy, Shannon information, functional information, semantic interpretation, coding convention)?
- Is "specified complexity" operationalized with a reference class, measurable target function, and selection/ascertainment correction?

## 5. Symmetrical comparative-evidence protocol

For each candidate account H, maintain a ledger with:
- **Scope:** exactly which transition(s) H claims to explain.
- **Antecedents:** initial chemistry, environment, informational prerequisites, agents, and constraints assumed.
- **Mechanism:** causal sequence, physically or agentically specified.
- **Evidence:** direct experiments, historical inference, simulation, analogy; primary provenance.
- **Closure:** required intermediate stages and which have been demonstrated.
- **Predictions:** prospective or risky discriminating observations.
- **Failures:** contrary observations, missing transitions, parameter sensitivity, auxiliary assumptions.
- **Likelihoods:** where defensible, P(E|H); otherwise mark unavailable.
- **Alternative accounts:** comparison on matched scope, not asymmetrical endpoints.

A naturalistic proposal receives no automatic causal credit for unspecified future chemistry. A design proposal receives no automatic causal credit for unspecified agency, timing, implementation or predicted signatures. Unknown does not mean false, impossible, or equiprobable.

### Comparative inference

Bayes factors require actual likelihoods: BF_DN = P(E|H_D) / P(E|H_N). Without specified H_D, H_N and defensible likelihoods, a numerical BF is unjustified. A qualitative comparative assessment can still identify demonstrated versus missing causal links, conditional predictions and evidential asymmetries.

Distinguish **model inadequacy**, **failure of a particular proposed pathway**, **impossibility of all naturalistic pathways**, and **positive support for design**. These are four different claims with different proof obligations.

## 6. Provisional mechanism families for adversarial review

Natural-origin families (not mutually exclusive): RNA-world / pre-RNA inheritance; metabolism-first / autocatalytic networks; lipid/protocell-first; hydrothermal/alkaline vent geochemistry; hybrid co-evolution of information, metabolism and compartments. These are research programmes with heterogeneous experimental support; no complete historical pathway is assumed.

Design families: direct origination of an initial functional biosystem; front-loaded chemical/physical conditions intended to produce life; guided or staged biological origination. Each needs an explicit observational signature, temporal and causal scope, and a way to distinguish itself from unspecified natural pathways. Generic "an intelligence could do this" is insufficient for comparative likelihood assessment.

## 7. Updated Fermi model

Let A denote origin of a viable biological lineage at a candidate site, T technological emergence conditional on A, C civilization trajectory, and D detection by an actual survey. Then:

P(D|X,Survey,H) = integral P(D|C,Survey,X,H) P(C|T,X,H) P(T|A,X,H) P(A|X,H) d(latent parameters),

where X includes stellar/planetary/galactic conditions. This is schematic marginalization; conditional terms require an explicit joint generative model and should not be treated as independent.

For biological technological civilizations, P(T and not A|X,H)=0 under the defined causal ontology. However, P(A|X,H) is not inferred solely from chemical complexity or from Earth's existence.

A design-origin account may change P(A|X,H) and its causal explanation; it does not eliminate the need to specify a distribution of design actions or locations before predicting extraterrestrial prevalence. Thus evidence for designed terrestrial life would not alone determine how many extraterrestrial biospheres exist.
