# WP-CD-0004: Examination, oddxian.com versus thinxai.net for the Designism estate's pages and pointers

**Status:** Chartered 2026-11-23, on JD Longmire's direction in the BWM alignment session. This is an examination and decision package. It authorizes no move; any transition it recommends is executed under a separate work package after JD decides.

## Question

Should the Designism estate's published pages and their pointers live under oddxian.com, where JD's public apologetics brand already sits, or remain under thinxai.net, the domain the newest sites were just stood up on?

## Current state, as inventoried 2026-11-23

- **worldmodel.thinxai.net**: the biblical-worldmodel site (GitHub Pages, workflow build), carrying the Biblical WorldModel presentation and BWM Illustrated.
- **designism.thinxai.net**: this repository's site, stood up under WP-CD-0003 on the same pattern.
- **jdlongmire.github.io/oddxian-apologetics**: the oddxian-apologetics Pages site (legacy build, no custom domain), carrying Illustrated Systematics and Apologetics Illustrated.
- **oddxian.com**: JD's Substack custom domain ("oddXian: Challenge the Consensus"). The apex is occupied by the publication, so Pages hosting under oddxian.com means subdomains, or displacing Substack, which is not presumed available.
- Pointers to these addresses are spread across repository READMEs, the repository index in this repo, site pages in two repos, and JD's wider corpus.

## What the examination must produce

1. **A complete inventory** of Designism-related pages and pointers: every Pages site, custom domain, and published URL, and every repository file that links to one.
2. **A comparison on stated criteria**, at minimum:
   - Brand coherence: oddXian is the faith and apologetics public brand; ThinxAI is the AI and company brand. Which domain should a reader associate with this programme?
   - The Substack constraint: oddxian.com's apex is taken; a move means subdomains (for example designism.oddxian.com, worldmodel.oddxian.com) and a decision about how they relate to the publication.
   - Continuity cost: every moved page breaks inbound links unless redirects are carried; GitHub Pages custom-domain changes do not redirect the old domain. The inventory must count the pointer surface that would need rewriting, in and beyond GitHub.
   - Zone control: thinxai.net and oddxian.com DNS sit in different arrangements; the examination names who can make each change.
3. **Options with a recommendation**: stay on thinxai.net; move to oddxian.com subdomains; or a hybrid (for example the governing and worldmodel sites under oddxian.com, tooling under thinxai.net), each costed against the inventory.
4. **A decision record** for JD: one question, the recommendation, and what executing it would charter next.

## Gate

Findings go to JD in this repository and in the session that chartered the package. No domain, Pages, or pointer changes are made under this package.
