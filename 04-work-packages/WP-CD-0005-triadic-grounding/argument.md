# TGA initial argument specification

Status: working draft; no theorem or empirical result claimed.

## Terms and premises
P1 (candidate ontological thesis): determinate dynamic actuality requires logical admissibility L, informational distinction I, and actualizing action A. Working notation: χ ≡ A(I | L). Verify typing and current TRT definitions upstream. Distinguish necessary conditions from sufficient conditions and static from dynamic actuality.
P2 (explanatory criterion): a candidate ultimate ground should account for the joint efficacy of these conditions, rather than merely name them.
P3 (epistemic criterion): a worldview should warrant the general reliability of reasoning used to assess it. Adaptive success alone does not entail truth tracking; evaluate naturalistic accounts of reliable inference rather than assuming their failure.
P4 (teleological criterion): if objective cosmic purpose exists, a candidate ground should explain it. Distinguish objective value, biological function, agent-relative ends and cosmic purpose; establish the relevant explanandum independently.
P5 (theistic proposal): the Christian Logos intrinsically possesses rationality, informational sufficiency and efficacious intentional agency, providing a proposed unified ground for actuality, intelligibility and purpose.
C1 (conditional abductive conclusion): insofar as P1–P5 are defensible and the alternatives examined supply less adequate grounding, the Logos has comparative explanatory advantage. This is not a deductive proof of God or Christian theology.

## Competitor register
Evaluate reductive and nonreductive naturalism, dispositional essentialism, Platonism plus actualization, impersonal necessary structure, brute integrated triad, idealism and non-Christian theism. Do not conflate atheism with physicalism. Distinguish brute facts from demonstrated necessity; both sides must justify claims of necessity. Compare scope, coherence, integration, independent warrant, assumptions and explanatory cost. Do not count hypothetical future explanations as completed work.

## Open proof obligations
O1: establish P1 without definitional circularity; test countermodels and distinguish logical necessity from metaphysical necessity.
O2: justify the demand for grounding and distinguish intrinsic unity from redescribed stipulation; test whether impersonal unified grounds suffice.
O3: reconstruct EAAN accurately and address reliabilism, evolutionary truth tracking, cultural correction and self-reference; theism also needs a bridge from divine rationality to actual cognitive reliability.
O4: establish objective purpose as an explanandum and test naturalist and non-theistic accounts.
O5: justify intentionality, personhood, necessity and the specifically Christian identification separately; avoid modal collapse and address evil and hiddenness as relevant comparative defeaters.
O6: apply Lakatos only with documented historical programme trajectories; metaphysical unification is not novel empirical corroboration. Distinguish methodological naturalism from metaphysical naturalism.
O7: establish novelty through primary literature; no priority claim at present.

## Defeat and revision
Withdraw or narrow C1 if a premise fails, a coherent countermodel defeats necessity, an alternative achieves comparable grounding at comparable cost, or the Logos proposal incurs unresolved contradictions that outweigh its benefits. Record unfavorable findings. Apply the same standard to all candidates.
