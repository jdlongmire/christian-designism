# Auxiliary Register

Lakatos's protective belt, made explicit. Every auxiliary hypothesis the programme has considered is recorded here with its disposition and the grounds for it, including auxiliaries the programme has **declined**.

The charter's claim to be applying Lakatosian criteria to itself rather than only to its rivals depends on this file being maintained honestly. An auxiliary quietly adopted under anomaly pressure and never logged is the exact failure mode the criterion exists to catch.

## Symmetry of auxiliary latitude (adopted 2026-11-15)

The programme claims the same latitude to introduce, hold and revise auxiliary hypotheses that the mainstream evolutionary research programme exercises, on the same terms. Adopted by JD; drafted by Claude.

**S1. Established mechanisms are shared.** An auxiliary that invokes an independently established mechanism is available to this programme on the same evidential terms as to the mainstream: it must be shown to operate in the case at hand, never merely assumed.

**S2. Framework-specific auxiliaries are graded alike.** Each programme may introduce auxiliaries drawn from its own background commitments: for the mainstream, deep time and rate uniformity; for this programme, designed process, fiat boundaries (HC-5; [I-0008](../issues/open/I-0008-ordinal-cardinal-amendment.md)) and corruption in a subjected creation. Both are graded by the same tests: Lakatos's ad hoc₁–₃ at the programme level, and McCullagh's criteria at the model level, with plausibility judged against each programme's own background ([I-0010](../issues/resolved/I-0010-erv-objective-abductive.md)).

**S3. Anomaly tolerance is symmetric.** A programme may carry open anomalies, and auxiliaries `UNDER REVIEW`, without abandonment, as Lakatos allows of programmes generally. Jorritsma (2022) judges the mainstream ERV programme progressive while recording several weakly supported auxiliaries. This programme is entitled to the same treatment.

**S4. Equal latitude means equal accounting.** Symmetry exempts nothing. Every auxiliary this programme uses is logged here and graded; the mainstream's are scored in the literature to the same standard.

**S5. Symmetric in principle, not in history.** The mainstream's tolerance of anomalies is backed by a record of corroborated novel facts (Jorritsma 2022). This programme's tolerance rests on being young. Lakatos's allowance for young programmes is real, and it does not substitute for corroboration (registered at [AD-002](ADVANCEMENT.md#ad-002--corroboration-of-a-prediction-whose-content-was-unavailable-at-registration)).

## A-001 – Functional maturity of origins

**Disposition:** `ADOPTED` (load-bearing)

A fiat act deploys a system in operational condition, and the physical state at deployment includes whatever inventory the system requires in order to function. Isotopic ratios in created rock are constitutive features of a working planet and carry chronological weight only for post-fiat processes.

**Grounds for adoption.** Two considerations distinguish this from the older appearance-of-age proposal. Functional necessity: long-lived radiogenic nuclides supply the internal heat driving mantle convection, plate tectonics, the geodynamo and the magnetosphere, quantified by geoneutrino measurement at roughly 20 TW from U-238 and Th-232 with a further ~4 TW from K-40 against 44.2 TW total terrestrial flux (Gando et al. 2011). A habitable planet requires the parent inventory, and charter §6.2 holds that a parent inventory entails daughter products. Second, the framing predicts cross-system concordance rather than accommodating it.

**Open liability 1 – daughter products.** The "entails daughter products" step is the auxiliary's weakest joint, and charter v1.1's own demarcation criterion appears to rule against it. Functional necessity as stated ("the system could not discharge its function at the moment of deployment without it") is satisfied by parent nuclides and not by radiogenic daughters, which are inert with respect to every function §6.2 names. If daughters read as elapsed history, the daughter-to-parent ratio is an ordinary clock and this auxiliary loses its central claim. Tracked at [I-0006](../issues/open/I-0006-daughter-products-fail-functional-necessity.md), which the charter's own rule requires be adjudicated rather than absorbed.

**Open liability 2 – concordance.** Integrated specification supports functional coherence among components; deriving concordance of *apparent ages* across systems with different half-lives and geochemistry is a more specific claim. Charter §8.3 v1.2 records the concession in strengthened form, separating the derivation weakness from liability 1, and stating that until liability 1 is settled the functional-maturity account of concordance is a consistent interpretation rather than an earned prediction. Liability 1 is the sharper of the two: if daughters read as elapsed history, concordance is exactly what elapsed process predicts and the framework offers no separate account.

*Candidate resolution pending.* [I-0008](../issues/open/I-0008-ordinal-cardinal-amendment.md) proposes replacing the integrated-specification argument entirely. On an ordinal commitment, concordance follows from there being a single map relating sequence to measure: every clock reparametrizes together, so concordance is preserved trivially and discordance would be the surprising result. That is a derivation where the present argument is an assertion. It also reduces liability 1's severity, since a daughter-to-parent ratio measuring a post-deployment interval is unproblematic for a programme that does not claim the metric integrates across the boundary. Awaiting author decision.

**Falsification condition.** Charter §9: continued failure to supply an operational demarcation criterion is fatal to this auxiliary on a longer timescale, and with it to the programme's geochronological position. Charter v1.1 supplies a candidate criterion, which moves [F-002](FALSIFIERS.md#f-002--continued-failure-to-supply-a-demarcation-criterion) forward and simultaneously raises liability 1.

## A-002 – Accelerated nuclear decay

**Disposition:** `DECLINED` as degenerating

Proposed by the RATE initiative (Vardiman, Snelling and Chaffin 2005) on evidence from helium retention in zircons, radiohalo populations, isochron discordance, and carbon-14 in samples expected to be radiocarbon-dead.

**Grounds for declining.**

1. **Thermal budget, unresolved by its own proponents.** Compressing the decay inventory into creation week and a Flood year leaves the released energy unchanged and raises the power. Order 10<sup>29</sup> J for five hundred million years of continental-crust decay, rising toward 10<sup>30</sup> J on fuller compressions.

2. **The constraint is disposal, not transport.** Shedding 10<sup>29</sup> to 10<sup>30</sup> J within one year requires the Earth's surface to radiate continuously at roughly 3,200 to 5,750 K, the upper figure approximating the solar photosphere. Silicate rock vaporizes near 3,000 K. No internal redistribution mechanism relieves this, because the bottleneck sits at the planetary radiative boundary. Full vaporization of the entire hydrosphere absorbs on the order of 3.5 × 10<sup>27</sup> J, one to two orders of magnitude short, and each cycle must still radiate past the same boundary.

3. **The proposed rescue is unmotivated and overshoots.** Volumetric cooling was introduced for no reason beyond removing the difficulty it was introduced to remove, which is the textbook signature of a degenerating adjustment. It also overshoots: cooling sufficient to preserve uranium-rich zircons would freeze the Flood waters.

4. **The programme incurs no cost by declining.** A-001 treats isotopic inventory as constitutive and therefore carries no obligation to compress elapsed decay at all.

**What is retained.** Declining accelerated decay does not dismiss RATE's observations. Two RATE-adjacent lines are live and are better served by this programme than by RATE's own auxiliary. See [`lines/geology.md`](../lines/geology.md) for the polonium-radiohalo transport convergence with hydraulic collapse, and [`issues/open/I-0004`](../issues/open/I-0004-helium-retention-tension.md) for the helium-retention tension the hydrotectonic model creates.

## A-003 – Hydraulic collapse (Global Flood Hydrotectonics)

**Disposition:** `ADOPTED`

Rapid continental reorganization proceeds by sudden failure of hydraulic seals in a fluid-rich pre-flood lithosphere, with continental and oceanic blocks hydroplaning on thin water films along shallow detachment horizons. Dissipation is relocated from viscous shear in mantle rock into the water film.

**Grounds for adoption.** Relocates dissipation into a medium where it can be disposed of, addressing the thermal difficulty that A-004 does not. Generates checkable claims about hydration textures, detachment geometry and thermal history.

**Correction (v1.1).** This entry previously read "supplies a mechanism for the global catastrophe with a thermal budget that closes." That was an overstatement introduced at repository creation and not by the author. Charter §9 v1.1 lists a closed hydrotectonic thermal budget among the programme's *advancement conditions* ([AD-003](ADVANCEMENT.md#ad-003--hydrotectonic-thermal-budget-closing-without-a-purpose-built-auxiliary)), which entails that it does not presently close. The charter's more cautious position governs. The adopted claim is that the mechanism makes closure reachable, not that closure is achieved.

**Status caveat.** Adopted at charter level while the source model is still in Phase 0 of its own roadmap. Its predictions are `DRAFT` in the register, not `REGISTERED`. See P-002.

## A-004 – Catastrophic plate tectonics

**Disposition:** `SUPERSEDED` by A-003 as to mechanism

Runaway subduction driven by thermal runaway instability in the mantle (Austin et al. 1994).

**Grounds.** Agreement with A-003 on the outcome, a single global reorganization compressed into months. Disagreement on mechanism. Driving rapid plate motion through accelerated mantle convection dissipates the released gravitational potential energy as viscous shear in mantle rock, and the resulting heat has no adequate disposal path. The thermal budget remains incomplete.

**Note.** Superseded as to mechanism, not dismissed as a body of work. Its claims about sediment volume and basin geometry remain relevant and are not in conflict with A-003.

## A-005 – Anisotropic synchrony convention

**Disposition:** `UNDER REVIEW`

Lisle (2010) argues that only the round-trip speed of light is measurable, that an observer-centric anisotropic synchrony convention reduces inward-directed light travel time to zero in the observer's frame, and that Scripture employs this convention.

**Why not yet adopted.** Substantive internal criticism regarding consistency with the invariance of the spacetime interval has not been adjudicated. The programme should not adopt an auxiliary whose internal critics it has not answered, and should not decline one it has not examined.

**Required to resolve.** A written adjudication of the interval-invariance objection, entered here, moving this to `ADOPTED` or `DECLINED`. Until then the light travel time problem is recorded as an open difficulty rather than a solved one, which is charter §6.5's position.

## A-006 – ERV model under methodological designism (non-human scope)

**Disposition:** `ADOPTED` (interim, scope-restricted; not load-bearing)

Shared endogenous retroviruses are read as real germline corruption events, propagated by copying and managed by a host maintenance architecture; some retroviral lineages may have arisen from host-regulated endogenous elements (H2b). Source: "Endogenous Retroviruses under Methodological Designism," working outline v0.4 (JD Longmire with Claude), not yet deposited in a repository of record.

**Scope.** Non-human lineages only, by the interim resolution of [I-0009](../issues/open/I-0009-erv-model-copied-history-vs-hc8.md). Shared human–ape ERVs are an open anomaly.

**Grounds for adoption.** It generates registrable content ([P-006](PREDICTIONS.md#p-006--h2b-exogenization-test-e5-foamy-virus-lineage)), with novelty marked at entry. By its own Lakatosian scorecard it currently reaches parity with the mainstream endogenization-amplification programme on coverage only, and has no corroborated novel fact.

**Falsification condition.** P-006 failing in the foamy lineage, with no other lineage passing by 2027-11-15, moves this to `UNDER REVIEW`.

## A-007 – Designed template copying at Adam's formation

**Disposition:** `SUPERSEDED` by [A-010](#a-010--common-deployment-two-population-erv-model) on 2026-11-15. Text below retained as filed.

Copied genomic history shared by humans and apes reflects the Designer's reuse of a template genome when Adam was formed, as distinct from reproduction. It is the only known route by which A-006 could extend to human lineages within [HC-8](../programme/hard-core.md).

**Why not adopted.** It currently has no excess empirical content over the mainstream programme, so adopting it under the pressure that prompted it would be ad hoc₁. It also carries stated costs: a pre-Adamic template population with its own sorting history inside the young timeframe, and the appearance-of-history question for the human case. See [I-0009](../issues/open/I-0009-erv-model-copied-history-vs-hc8.md).

**Required to adopt.** A registered prediction that distinguishes template copying from reproduction, with novelty marked at entry, and the exegetical note required by I-0009.

**Abductive assessment (added 2026-11-15, outline v0.5 §6F; see [I-0010](../issues/resolved/I-0010-erv-objective-abductive.md)).** On McCullagh's criteria A-007 gains scope and power. It loses on ad hocness (a pre-Adamic template population that sorted variation; a new category of elapsed history "inherited by fiat copy" that functional necessity does not cover) and on disconfirmation by the framework's own accepted beliefs. By [I-0011](../issues/resolved/I-0011-ervs-as-functional-necessity-specimen-class.md), the copied ERVs are elapsed history, so their insertion and damage precede the Fall, against the Fall-linked reading of corruption and in tension with HC-6 (Gen 1:31). Claude's verdict, **adopted by JD 2026-11-15 with the risk accepted:** A-007 does not close the scope gap at acceptable cost and is not adopted. Under S3 it is retained `UNDER REVIEW`, as an available auxiliary, and is not declined. What could change it: an account of pre-Fall corruption in non-human lineages held on independent grounds, or a reading of HC-6 compatible with it.

## A-008 – Independent targeting of shared integration sites

**Disposition:** `DECLINED` as refuted

Proposed in the July 2025 ERV draft and widely in the design-side literature: retroviruses independently target the same "integration interfaces" or hotspots in related genomes, producing shared ERVs without copying.

**Grounds.** Integration preferences are real and regional, at most kilobase scale (Schröder et al. 2002; Wu et al. 2003). A single retrovirus independently infecting closely related species landed at non-orthologous sites about 95.8% of the time (Yohn et al. 2005). Empty pre-integration sites carry one copy of the target sequence and occupied sites two (Barbulescu et al. 1999, 2001), so the duplication is made by insertion. Shared duplications plus shared later mutations at the same base pair require a single event that was then copied.

## A-009 – Creation in place and originally site-specific integration

**Disposition:** `DECLINED` as refuted

Two related proposals: ERVs were created in strategic genomic locations (Liu 2013, ICC); or ERV integration was originally designed as site- or sequence-specific (Borger 2009).

**Grounds.** An element created in place has no reason to carry a duplication of its own target site, and the empty-site evidence shows insertion produces it; creation in place also reintroduces the appearance-of-history objection. Original site-specificity would leave matching duplications, but it fails on shared post-insertion changes nested across species (Johnson & Coffin 1999), on the HERV-K-GC1 sorting case, and on present-day integration behaviour, and it posits a lost specificity with no independent evidence. A consensus of human HERV-K copies reconstructs an infectious progenitor that integrates with the present-day signature (Dewannieux et al. 2006).

## A-010 – Common deployment (two-population ERV model)

**Disposition:** `UNDER REVIEW`. Supersedes A-007. JD's stated model (session of 2026-11-15); written up by Claude. Not adopted: it has no registered prediction yet, so adopting it now would be ad hoc₁ by the standard applied to A-007.

**Statement.** ERV-class sequence in human and ape genomes belongs to two populations.

1. **Deployed loci.** Good code installed by the Designer at instantiation, at the same addresses in kinds that share the relevant architecture. Viruses and virus-like elements were originally functional components of a good, ecologically active creation. These loci are expected to be shared across kinds, nested, and functional.
2. **Post-Fall loci.** Products of disruption after the Fall, including cross-kind transmission ("kind-jumps") and ordinary untargeted integration. These are expected to be lineage-specific, scattered, and often neutral or harmful.

Humans share deployed loci with apes by common deployment, with no reproductive descent of humans from any ape kind. Descent within an ape kind's own branches is accepted.

**Author's framing commitments (JD, 2026-11-15).** Non-predatory animal death before the Fall, with scavenging acceptable; only humans initially immortal; common descent denied for humans only. See the HC-8 scope proposal in [I-0009](../issues/open/I-0009-erv-model-copied-history-vs-hc8.md).

**Fit with data gathered 2026-11-15** (fit only; common descent explains each item too):
- PtERV1 infected the gorilla and chimpanzee lineages separately, about 540 loci, with under 4% positional overlap and one orthologous insertion reported between them (Yohn et al. 2005; later counts as cited in the 2026 *Science Advances* LINC00662 study). This is the expected signature of a post-Fall kind-jump and is the model's type specimen for population 2.
- Human absence of PtERV1 and of HERV-K-GC1 needs no sorting population: humans were never in those branches.
- Human-specific and polymorphic HML-2 insertions (113 human-specific against 440 shared with chimpanzee; Belshaw et al. 2005) are population 2 in the human line.

**How it differs from the declined A-009, and which refuting grounds it answers.**

| A-009 ground | Status under A-010 |
|---|---|
| A created element has no reason to carry a target-site duplication | Answered only if deployment ran the integration machinery (mode D-a below). Unanswered under specification in place (mode D-b). |
| Present-day integration is untargeted | Answered. No natural site-specificity is claimed; shared addresses come from a design act, and natural integration is assigned to population 2. |
| HERV-K-GC1 sorting | Answered by the HC-8 scope clarification. |
| Shared post-insertion changes nested across species (Johnson & Coffin 1999) | **Not answered.** See cost 2. |
| A lost specificity with no independent evidence | Replaced by a design act, which also has no independent evidence. See cost 5. |
| Infectious progenitor reconstructed from HERV-K copies (Dewannieux et al. 2006) | Partly answered: consistent with deployed code that was originally active. It cuts against reading shared lesions as specification. |

**Deployment mode (open).** D-a: the code was installed by running integration, so the flanking duplication is a real process mark of the deployment act. D-b: the loci were specified in place. Claude leans D-a, because D-b inherits A-009's first ground. JD has not ruled.

**Costs, stated in advance.**

1. **Reassignment under pressure.** [I-0011](../issues/resolved/I-0011-ervs-as-functional-necessity-specimen-class.md) classed ERVs as process residue, and AD-001 counts that verdict on the condition that it "holds without reassignment". A-010 reassigns the shared set to the initial state. Adopting it spends that conjunct and must be logged as a reassignment made after an unwelcome verdict.
2. **Shared lesions.** Humans and chimpanzees carry identical disabling changes at shared loci. They cannot be independent post-Fall decay. The model must read them as specified, which predicts patterned lesions (regulatory parts preserved) against the scatter expected from neutral decay. If the pattern is scatter, this counts against.
3. **LTR divergence gradient.** ~~Unexplained.~~ **Retired 2026-11-16** by JD's chronometry argument: divergence is a property of a coherently instantiated mature state rather than a readout of elapsed process, with chronometers agreeing because they are downstream of one instantiation. The same move covers much of the target-site-duplication objection. See [session record](../programme/session-record-2026-11-16.md) section 3.
4. **The deployment variable is unidentified.** Deployment must follow something that groups humans with chimpanzees against gorillas, since humans and chimpanzees share loci gorillas lack. Ecology does not (chimpanzees and gorillas are closer). Gross anatomy does not either (same). Defining the variable from the genomes themselves would be circular. An independent variable is required.
5. **No excess content yet.** Parity on the shared-locus datum, nothing more, until a prediction is registered and survives.
6. **Post-Fall rates.** Population 2 must fit the young timeframe: hundreds of lineage-specific insertions, and LTR divergence near 1% in PtERV1, which standard rates read as millions of years. Needs a rate account.
7. **Entailment.** Under the functional-maturity criterion the initial state contains only what function requires, so the deployed set must be functionally required. Most shared loci are untested.

**Framing (JD, 2026-11-16).** A mature designed forest rather than an emergent tree: kinds instantiated complete, descent operating within each, and vines (lateral transfer, population 2) traversing the forest afterwards. The dispute with common descent is about the maturity of the starting point.

**Candidate predictions (not registered; novelty and mainstream expectation to be checked first).**
- *Discontinuity at kind boundaries.* Between-kind genomic distances cluster with real discontinuity; the emergent-tree model expects continuous gradation. The first candidate in this programme that could add content rather than absorb a result. Sharpest, and most strained, at the human-ape boundary.
- *Function step.* Function among shared loci is flat across nesting depth, with a step down at lineage-specific loci. Descent with co-option expects function to rise with insertion age.
- *Lesion patterning.* Disabling changes at shared loci are non-randomly placed, sparing regulatory sequence.

**Required to adopt.** At least one of these registered with novelty marked at entry; cost 1 logged against AD-001; cost 4 resolved or carried explicitly; JD's ruling on deployment mode; the HC-8 scope text settled.

*Source confidence:* counts are from primary papers read in search extracts on 2026-11-15, `MEDIUM`; author attributions for Belshaw et al. 2005 and Yohn et al. 2005 to be verified against the papers before citation elsewhere.
