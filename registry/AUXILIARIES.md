# Auxiliary Register

Lakatos's protective belt, made explicit. Every auxiliary hypothesis the programme has considered is recorded here with its disposition and the grounds for it, including auxiliaries the programme has **declined**.

The charter's claim to be applying Lakatosian criteria to itself rather than only to its rivals depends on this file being maintained honestly. An auxiliary quietly adopted under anomaly pressure and never logged is the exact failure mode the criterion exists to catch.

## Disposition vocabulary

| Disposition | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ADOPTED` | Part of the programme's current protective belt. |
| `DECLINED` | Available to the programme and refused, with grounds. |
| `UNDER REVIEW` | Neither adopted nor refused; open question. |
| `SUPERSEDED` | Replaced by a later auxiliary. |

## A-001 – Functional maturity of origins

**Disposition:** `ADOPTED` (load-bearing)

A fiat act deploys a system in operational condition, and the physical state at deployment includes whatever inventory the system requires in order to function. Isotopic ratios in created rock are constitutive features of a working planet and carry chronological weight only for post-fiat processes.

**Grounds for adoption.** Two considerations distinguish this from the older appearance-of-age proposal. Functional necessity: long-lived radiogenic nuclides supply the internal heat driving mantle convection, plate tectonics, the geodynamo and the magnetosphere, quantified by geoneutrino measurement at roughly 20 TW from U-238 and Th-232 with a further ~4 TW from K-40 against 44.2 TW total terrestrial flux (Gando et al. 2011). A habitable planet requires the parent inventory, and charter §6.2 holds that a parent inventory entails daughter products. Second, the framing predicts cross-system concordance rather than accommodating it.

**Open liability 1 – daughter products.** The "entails daughter products" step is the auxiliary's weakest joint, and charter v1.1's own demarcation criterion appears to rule against it. Functional necessity as stated ("the system could not discharge its function at the moment of deployment without it") is satisfied by parent nuclides and not by radiogenic daughters, which are inert with respect to every function §6.2 names. If daughters read as elapsed history, the daughter-to-parent ratio is an ordinary clock and this auxiliary loses its central claim. Tracked at [I-0006](../issues/open/I-0006-daughter-products-fail-functional-necessity.md), which the charter's own rule requires be adjudicated rather than absorbed.

**Open liability 2 – concordance.** Integrated specification supports functional coherence among components; deriving concordance of *apparent ages* across systems with different half-lives and geochemistry is a more specific claim. Charter §8.3 v1.2 records the concession in strengthened form, separating the derivation weakness from liability 1, and stating that until liability 1 is settled the functional-maturity account of concordance is a consistent interpretation rather than an earned prediction. Liability 1 is the sharper of the two: if daughters read as elapsed history, concordance is exactly what elapsed process predicts and the framework offers no separate account.

*Candidate resolution pending.* [I-0008](../issues/open/I-0008-ordinal-cardinal-amendment.md) proposes replacing the integrated-specification argument entirely. On an ordinal commitment, concordance follows from there being a single map relating sequence to measure: every clock reparametrizes together, so concordance is preserved trivially and discordance would be the surprising result. That is a derivation where the present argument is an assertion. It also reduces liability 1's severity, since a daughter-to-parent ratio measuring a post-deployment interval is unproblematic for a programme that does not claim the metric integrates across the boundary. Awaiting author decision.

**Falsification condition.** Charter §9: continued failure to supply an operational demarcation criterion is fatal to this auxiliary on a longer timescale, and with it to the programme's geochronological position. Charter v1.1 supplies a candidate criterion, which moves [F-002](FALSIFIERS.md#f-002--continued-failure-to-supply-a-demarcation-criterion) forward and simultaneously raises liability 1.

## A-002 – Accelerated nuclear decay

**Disposition:** `DECLINED` as degenerating

Proposed by the RATE initiative (Vardiman, Snelling and Chaffin 2005) on evidence from helium retention in zircons, radiohalo populations, isochron discordance, and carbon-14 in samples expected to be radiocarbon-dead.

**Grounds for declining.**

1. **Thermal budget, unresolved by its own proponents.** Compressing the decay inventory into creation week and a Flood year leaves the released energy unchanged and raises the power. Order 10<sup>29</sup> J for five hundred million years of continental-crust decay, rising toward 10<sup>30</sup> J on fuller compressions.

2. **The constraint is disposal, not transport.** Shedding 10<sup>29</sup> to 10<sup>30</sup> J within one year requires the Earth's surface to radiate continuously at roughly 3,200 to 5,750 K, the upper figure approximating the solar photosphere. Silicate rock vaporizes near 3,000 K. No internal redistribution mechanism relieves this, because the bottleneck sits at the planetary radiative boundary. Full vaporization of the entire hydrosphere absorbs on the order of 3.5 × 10<sup>27</sup> J, one to two orders of magnitude short, and each cycle must still radiate past the same boundary.

3. **The proposed rescue is unmotivated and overshoots.** Volumetric cooling was introduced for no reason beyond removing the difficulty it was introduced to remove, which is the textbook signature of a degenerating adjustment. It also overshoots: cooling sufficient to preserve uranium-rich zircons would freeze the Flood waters.

4. **The programme incurs no cost by declining.** A-001 treats isotopic inventory as constitutive and therefore carries no obligation to compress elapsed decay at all.

**What is retained.** Declining accelerated decay does not dismiss RATE's observations. Two RATE-adjacent lines are live and are better served by this programme than by RATE's own auxiliary. See [`lines/geology.md`](../lines/geology.md) for the polonium-radiohalo transport convergence with hydraulic collapse, and [`issues/open/I-0004`](../issues/open/I-0004-helium-retention-tension.md) for the helium-retention tension the hydrotectonic model creates.

## A-003 – Hydraulic collapse (Global Flood Hydrotectonics)

**Disposition:** `ADOPTED`

Rapid continental reorganization proceeds by sudden failure of hydraulic seals in a fluid-rich pre-flood lithosphere, with continental and oceanic blocks hydroplaning on thin water films along shallow detachment horizons. Dissipation is relocated from viscous shear in mantle rock into the water film.

**Grounds for adoption.** Relocates dissipation into a medium where it can be disposed of, addressing the thermal difficulty that A-004 does not. Generates checkable claims about hydration textures, detachment geometry and thermal history.

**Correction (v1.1).** This entry previously read "supplies a mechanism for the global catastrophe with a thermal budget that closes." That was an overstatement introduced at repository creation and not by the author. Charter §9 v1.1 lists a closed hydrotectonic thermal budget among the programme's *advancement conditions* ([AD-003](ADVANCEMENT.md#ad-003--hydrotectonic-thermal-budget-closing-without-a-purpose-built-auxiliary)), which entails that it does not presently close. The charter's more cautious position governs. The adopted claim is that the mechanism makes closure reachable, not that closure is achieved.

**Status caveat.** Adopted at charter level while the source model is still in Phase 0 of its own roadmap. Its predictions are `DRAFT` in the register, not `REGISTERED`. See P-002.

## A-004 – Catastrophic plate tectonics

**Disposition:** `SUPERSEDED` by A-003 as to mechanism

Runaway subduction driven by thermal runaway instability in the mantle (Austin et al. 1994).

**Grounds.** Agreement with A-003 on the outcome, a single global reorganization compressed into months. Disagreement on mechanism. Driving rapid plate motion through accelerated mantle convection dissipates the released gravitational potential energy as viscous shear in mantle rock, and the resulting heat has no adequate disposal path. The thermal budget remains incomplete.

**Note.** Superseded as to mechanism, not dismissed as a body of work. Its claims about sediment volume and basin geometry remain relevant and are not in conflict with A-003.

## A-005 – Anisotropic synchrony convention

**Disposition:** `UNDER REVIEW`

Lisle (2010) argues that only the round-trip speed of light is measurable, that an observer-centric anisotropic synchrony convention reduces inward-directed light travel time to zero in the observer's frame, and that Scripture employs this convention.

**Why not yet adopted.** Substantive internal criticism regarding consistency with the invariance of the spacetime interval has not been adjudicated. The programme should not adopt an auxiliary whose internal critics it has not answered, and should not decline one it has not examined.

**Required to resolve.** A written adjudication of the interval-invariance objection, entered here, moving this to `ADOPTED` or `DECLINED`. Until then the light travel time problem is recorded as an open difficulty rather than a solved one, which is charter §6.5's position.

## Standing assessment

Two adopted, one declined, one superseded, one under review.

The declined entry is the programme's principal evidence that it applies the Lakatosian criterion to itself. It should not be the only one. An auxiliary register in which nothing is ever declined after A-002 would indicate the criterion had stopped being applied.
