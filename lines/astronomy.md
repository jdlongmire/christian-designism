## Position

Two separable pieces of work sit under this line, and the charter treats them very differently.

**The light travel time problem.** The charter calls it "the sharpest challenge to the programme," which is the right assessment. Relativistic treatments invoking gravitational time dilation and conventionalist treatments exploiting the unmeasurability of the one-way speed of light are both in play. Lisle (2010) develops the conventionalist route. The programme has not adopted it, because the internal criticism regarding consistency with the invariance of the spacetime interval has not been adjudicated. Recorded as an open difficulty, not a solved problem.

**The cosmological pre-registration.** Charter §7 cites a pre-registered prediction concerning the persistence of the H0 and S8 tensions under single-parameter extensions, with falsifiers committed in advance and a resolution date, and calls it "the correct instrument for the purpose."

## Status against the charter

**The pre-registration document has not been located.** This is the second-largest traceability gap in the programme, after the hydrotectonics falsifier list.

The charter conditions this prediction's value on its being "adjudicated rather than revised." An unlocated pre-registration can be neither adjudicated nor shown not to have been revised, so the citation currently does no work. The honest resolutions are, in order of preference:

1. Recover the original document with its timestamp and deposit it here verbatim, then register P-003 with its original falsifiers and date
2. Failing recovery, re-register from today with new falsifiers and a new resolution date, and drop the priority claim
3. Failing both, remove the citation from charter §7
