# Line: Genetics

**Repository of record:** none. External literature line rather than an in-programme model.
**Charter section:** [§6.4](../charter/consilience-is-not-consensus.md#64-genetics)
**Predictions:** [P-004](../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-004--genomic-degradation-constrains-elapsed-generational-time) (`DRAFT`); [P-006](../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-006--h2b-exogenization-test-e5-foamy-virus-lineage) (`REGISTERED`, resolves 2027-11-15)
**Open issues:** [I-0009](../issues/open/I-0009-erv-model-copied-history-vs-hc8.md)

## Position

Mutational load: deleterious mutations accumulate faster than purifying selection removes them, so genomes degrade over generational time (Sanford 2005). If sustained, this constrains the duration over which extant genomes can have persisted, and the constraint runs toward shorter chronologies.

## Status against the charter

The charter's handling is correct in posture and thin in content. It records that the argument is contested within population genetics and that the contest is empirical rather than definitional, which is the right location for it and an honest thing to say about a line the programme leans on.

What the charter does not do, and what this line needs, is state the argument in a form that could fail. The load argument's force depends on selection efficiency against low-effect-size deleterious mutations, and that is a measurable quantity with an active mainstream literature arguing the opposite conclusion. A programme that cites the line without engaging that literature is not entitled to the inference.

## What registration requires

1. A quantitative statement: the selection-efficiency threshold above which the load argument fails
2. The measurement that would establish that threshold has been crossed
3. A written engagement with the principal mainstream objections, entered here, rather than a citation to Sanford alone

This is the line where the programme is most exposed to the charge of citing a favourable result without adjudicating its critics, which is the same failure the charter correctly identifies in the wider creationist literature over accelerated decay. The standard applied to A-002 applies here.

## Sub-line: endogenous retroviruses (added 2026-11-15)

**Model:** "Endogenous Retroviruses under Methodological Designism," working outline v0.4. *Not yet deposited in a repository of record.*

**Position.** The ERV record is read as real corruption events, propagated by copying and managed by a host maintenance architecture. The model sets its own burden at **explanatory parity, at least**, with the mainstream endogenization-amplification programme, scored on Lakatosian criteria. Jorritsma (2022) has already assessed the mainstream programme as progressive on orthologous ERVs, target site duplications and LTR divergence.

**Honest standing.** On the outline's own scorecard the model reaches parity on coverage only, and only by incorporating the mainstream programme's auxiliaries. It has no corroborated novel fact. Two of its earlier mechanisms (independent targeting of hotspots; creation in place) are retired as refuted.

**Registered content.** [P-006](../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-006--h2b-exogenization-test-e5-foamy-virus-lineage): the H2b exogenization test, foamy-virus lineage first. Novelty `PARTIAL` (C1 known; C2 and C3 novel).

**Open difficulty.** [I-0009](../issues/open/I-0009-erv-model-copied-history-vs-hc8.md): the model's acceptance of copied history across the human–ape boundary conflicts with HC-8. Interim resolution: the model is adopted for non-human lineages only ([A-006](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-006--erv-model-under-methodological-designism-non-human-scope)), shared human–ape ERVs are an open anomaly, and designed template copying is under review ([A-007](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-007--designed-template-copying-at-adams-formation)). Two design-side mechanisms are declined as refuted ([A-008](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-008--independent-targeting-of-shared-integration-sites), [A-009](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-009--creation-in-place-and-originally-site-specific-integration)).

**Objective and appraisal** ([I-0010](../issues/resolved/I-0010-erv-objective-abductive.md)): a plausible, abductive model within the designist framework. On McCullagh's criteria it reaches parity with the mainstream model except on scope, where shared human–ape ERVs remain the one open class. Lakatosian standing is still reported separately.

**Demarcation** ([I-0011](../issues/resolved/I-0011-ervs-as-functional-necessity-specimen-class.md)): functional necessity applied to ERVs supplies AD-001's third clean specimen class, with an unwelcome verdict, and a candidate process-signature rule for the criterion's open difficulty 3 (accepted 2026-11-15).

**Auxiliary latitude:** the programme claims the same latitude as the mainstream programme, on the same terms (symmetry rule, [`registry/AUXILIARIES.md`](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#symmetry-of-auxiliary-latitude-adopted-2026-11-15)). A-007 is retained `UNDER REVIEW` under that rule.
