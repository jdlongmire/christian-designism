# Line: Paleontology

**Repository of record:** none.
**Charter section:** [§6.3](../charter/consilience-is-not-consensus.md#63-paleontology)
**Predictions:** [P-005](../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-005--ecological-zonation-predicts-stratigraphic-position) (`DRAFT`)

## Position

Fossil succession as a record of burial order under catastrophic conditions: pre-catastrophe ecological zonation, hydrodynamic sorting, differential mobility, and inundation sequence, with post-catastrophe diversification accounting for later assemblages.

## Status against the charter

This is the **least developed line in the programme**, and the charter's one-paragraph treatment reflects rather than conceals that. The account is stated qualitatively and generates no advance commitment in its present form. Charter §6.3 says the framework "predicts patterns" without naming a pattern, a taxon set, or a distribution that would count against it.

That is not a defect of honesty; it is a defect of content. The line should either be operationalized or the charter should stop listing it among the disciplines across which the programme "has produced testable content," since on this line it has not.

## What operationalization requires

1. A named taxon set with an independently measurable ecological-zonation proxy
2. A quantitative prediction relating that proxy to stratigraphic position
3. The distribution that would count against the account
4. A specified dataset against which the test would run

Until at least the first three exist, P-005 stays `DRAFT` and this line contributes to the programme's consistency but not to its predictive content.
