# I-0004 – Hydrotectonics creates tension with helium retention in zircons

**Opened:** 2026-08-02
**Severity:** Medium. A self-generated difficulty, which is the kind worth publishing.
**Related:** [A-002](../../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-002--accelerated-nuclear-decay), [A-003](../../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-003--hydraulic-collapse-global-flood-hydrotectonics), [`lines/geology.md`](../../lines/geology.md)

## Problem

Helium retention in zircons is the strongest quantitative result in the RATE corpus. The argument depends on measured diffusion coefficients and therefore on the thermal and fluid history of the sampled rock.

Global Flood Hydrotectonics, adopted as A-003, places a hot, fluid-saturated, hydrothermally active crust in exactly the relevant window. Two effects follow, both adverse to retention:

- Elevated temperature accelerates helium diffusion out of the zircon lattice
- Pervasive fluid flow through channeled porosity supplies an advection path removing helium from the surrounding medium, steepening the concentration gradient

A counter is available. If pore fluids were themselves helium-saturated at high pressure, the external boundary condition changes and retention could survive or improve. A version of this argument has been made in response to critics of the retention result.

## Why this is the programme's problem rather than RATE's

The programme has declined accelerated decay (A-002) and therefore does not need the helium result. But it has adopted a geological model that *sets the boundary condition* on which the result depends. Whether the counter works is now a question about A-003's fluid chemistry, not about RATE's diffusion measurements.

Left unexamined, a critic gets to say that this programme's own geology falsifies a young-earth result the wider movement relies on. That is a fair charge and the programme should get there first.

## What resolution requires

1. Estimate helium partial pressure in the pore fluid under the model's own pressure, temperature and flux conditions
2. Determine whether that boundary condition supports or degrades retention relative to the measurements
3. Record the result either way, including if it is unfavourable

## Note on the wider point

There is a smaller tension of the same shape. Pervasive fluid circulation makes secondary carbon contamination pathways more plausible, which pushes against the in-situ reading of carbon-14 in radiocarbon-dead samples. Lower stakes, same structure, worth a sentence when this is written up.

## Status

Open. Belongs in the upstream model's defense materials as well as here.
