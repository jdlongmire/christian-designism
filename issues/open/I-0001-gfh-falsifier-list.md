# I-0001 – GFH falsifier list blocks P-002 registration

**Opened:** 2026-08-02
**Severity:** High. Largest traceability gap in the programme.
**Blocks:** [P-002](../../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-002--hydration-textures-required-by-global-flood-hydrotectonics), [F-003](../../registry/FALSIFIERS.md#f-003--systematic-failure-of-registered-predictions)
**Upstream repo:** `jdlongmire/global-flood-hydrotectonic-model`

## Problem

Charter §7 cites Global Flood Hydrotectonics as making "specific and checkable claims about hydration textures," and §9 makes the absence of those textures a condition of abandonment. Both statements treat the prediction as registered and adjudicable.

It is neither. The upstream model is in Phase 0 of its own eight-phase roadmap, and the two outstanding Phase-0 deliverables are a versioned assumptions table and a one-page falsifier list. The falsifier list is exactly the artifact charter §9 is drawing on, and it does not exist.

## Why this matters more than a missing document

The charter's central claim to scientific standing is that it specifies enforceable falsifiers. F-003 makes registered-prediction failure a condition of abandonment. If no prediction is registered, F-003 cannot trigger, and the charter's §9 is decorative on this point.

A critic who checks the upstream repository will find this in a few minutes. Better to have recorded it first.

## Resolution paths

**Preferred.** Land the GFH one-page falsifier list upstream, then register P-002 here with a named detachment horizon, texture class, and sampling criterion. This closes the gap in the direction that increases the programme's content.

**Acceptable interim.** Amend charter §§7 and 9 to read "stated, pending registration" for the hydration-texture prediction. Honest, and it costs the charter one of its two live prediction claims until the upstream work lands.

**Not acceptable.** Leaving the charter as written while the register shows `DRAFT`.

## Status

Open. Interim disclosure is in place: PREDICTIONS.md, FALSIFIERS.md and lines/geology.md all record the gap explicitly, and the registry's standing assessment names it.
