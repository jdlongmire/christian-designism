# I-0003 – Cosmological pre-registration not located

**Opened:** 2026-08-02
**Severity:** High.
**Blocks:** [P-003](../../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-003--persistence-of-the-h0-and-s8-tensions-under-single-parameter-extensions)

## Problem

Charter §7 states:

> A pre-registered cosmological prediction concerning the persistence of the H0 and S8 tensions under single-parameter extensions, with falsifiers committed in advance and a resolution date, is the correct instrument for the purpose, and its value depends on its being adjudicated rather than revised.

A search of the author's public repositories has not located this document. Without it, the following are all unknown: the exact claim, the committed falsifiers, the resolution date, and the commitment timestamp.

## Why the citation currently does no work

The charter itself supplies the reason. The prediction's value is conditioned on being "adjudicated rather than revised." An unlocated document can be neither adjudicated nor shown not to have been revised. A pre-registration that cannot be produced on request is functionally indistinguishable from a claim made after the fact, and the programme's own standard treats that distinction as decisive.

## Resolution paths, in order of preference

1. **Recover and deposit.** Locate the original with a verifiable timestamp (repository commit, Zenodo deposit, OSF registration, dated correspondence) and deposit it here verbatim. Register P-003 with its original falsifiers and date.
2. **Re-register from today.** If recovery fails, state the prediction fresh with new falsifiers and a new resolution date, and drop the priority claim. The prediction remains valuable; the "pre-registered" framing does not survive.
3. **Remove the citation.** If neither, strike the sentence from charter §7. The section's argument does not depend on it.

Option 2 is not a defeat. A live registered forecast with a real resolution date is worth more to the programme than an unverifiable priority claim.

Open. P-003 held at `DRAFT`.
