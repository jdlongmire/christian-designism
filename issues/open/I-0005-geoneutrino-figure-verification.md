# I-0005 – Verify geoneutrino figures against the primary source

**Opened:** 2026-08-02
**Severity:** Low, but the claim is load-bearing for A-001's strongest argument.
**Related:** [A-001](../../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-001--functional-maturity-of-origins), charter §6.2

## Problem

Charter §6.2 states:

> KamLAND data combined with Borexino attribute 20.0 TW to combined uranium-238 and thorium-232 decay, with potassium-40 emission below current detection thresholds though known to add roughly 4 TW, against a total terrestrial heat flux of 44.2 TW (Gando et al. 2011).

The figures are plausible and consistent with the standard literature. Three points need checking against the primary before this is quoted in a published charter:

1. **The 20.0 TW attribution.** Whether that specific value comes from the combined KamLAND and Borexino analysis reported in Gando et al. (2011), and whether the stated precision is theirs. Reported radiogenic heat estimates carry substantial uncertainty ranges, and a single-value citation without the range overstates the measurement.

2. **The 44.2 TW total.** This figure traces to Pollack, Hurter and Johnson (1993) rather than to Gando, and total terrestrial heat flux estimates have been revised since, with a competing figure near 47 TW in circulation. Citing it to Gando attributes it to the wrong source.

3. **The K-40 contribution.** "Roughly 4 TW" is standard, but it derives from compositional modeling rather than from measurement, and the charter's phrasing ("known to add") is stronger than the evidential basis supports. "Estimated" would be accurate.

## Why it matters

The functional-necessity argument is the strongest component of A-001, and it is strong precisely because it is quantitative. A quantitative argument built on a citation that misattributes one figure and overstates the precision of another invites exactly the attack the charter is otherwise well defended against.

This is a small correction that protects a good argument.

## Resolution

Read Gando et al. (2011) directly, restate each figure with its uncertainty range and correct attribution, and record the corrections here. Add Pollack et al. (1993) or the current preferred source to the reference list if the total-flux figure is retained.

## Status

Open. Charter text unchanged pending verification, since the figures are more likely right than wrong and speculative amendment would be worse than a tracked check.
