# I-0002 – Pearson priority claim requires verification

**Opened:** 2026-08-02
**Severity:** Medium. Determines whether P-001 is a prediction or an accommodation.
**Blocks:** [P-001](../../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-001--pre-loaded-deep-water-budget-in-the-mantle-transition-zone)

## Problem

Charter §7 offers the first terrestrial ringwoodite (Pearson et al. 2014, a diamond inclusion from Juína indicating a locally hydrous transition zone at roughly 1 wt% water) as corroboration of functional maturity's expectation of a pre-loaded deep-water budget, "where mainstream geochemical expectation had been considerably lower."

The charter already flags this: "The priority claim here requires verification of the dating of pre-2014 sources before it is stated as firm, and it is offered subject to that check."

The check has not been done.

## Why it is load-bearing

This is the programme's only claimed corroboration. Under the Lakatosian criterion the charter adopts, an adjustment that predicts a novel fact subsequently corroborated is progressive, and one that accommodates a fact already known is degenerating. Which of those this is depends entirely on what the programme committed to, and when, relative to 2014.

Two things need establishing, and they are separable:

1. **What mainstream expectation actually was pre-2014.** The transition-zone water content literature is substantial and predates Pearson considerably; wadsleyite and ringwoodite hydration capacity was studied experimentally well before a natural sample was recovered. The claim that expectation was "considerably lower" may be true of *observed* hydration and false of *predicted capacity*, which are different claims and only one of them supports the priority.

2. **What this programme committed to, and when.** A functional-maturity expectation of a pre-loaded water budget articulated after 2014 is not a prediction of the 2014 result regardless of how natural the expectation is.

## What honest resolution looks like

If (1) and (2) both hold, register P-001 with the documentary evidence attached. If either fails, downgrade the claim in charter §7 from corroboration to consistency, which is a much weaker and still true statement.

The programme applies this standard to accelerated decay in A-002. It applies here.

## Status

Open. P-001 held at `DRAFT` pending resolution.
