# I-0010 – ERV sub-line objective clarified: abductive model, Lakatosian standing retained as accountability

**Opened:** 2026-11-15
**Severity:** Medium. Methodological; changes how the ERV sub-line is appraised.
**Type:** Clarification by JD, recorded with the yardstick change made explicit.
**Related:** [A-006](../../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-006--erv-model-under-methodological-designism-non-human-scope), [A-007](../../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-007--designed-template-copying-at-adams-formation), [I-0009](../open/I-0009-erv-model-copied-history-vs-hc8.md), [I-0011](I-0011-ervs-as-functional-necessity-specimen-class.md), [P-006](../../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-006--h2b-exogenization-test-e5-foamy-virus-lineage)

## What changed

JD (2026-11-15): the objective of the ERV work is to establish a **plausible, abductive explanatory model within the designist framework**.

The working outline had defined the model's burden ("explanatory parity, at least") using Lakatos's programme-appraisal criteria: coverage, auxiliary discipline, novel corroborated facts, heuristic fertility. On those the model fell short on three of four. Claude's assessment is that this mislocated the burden. Lakatos's criteria measure whether a research programme is progressing; whether a model is a good explanation is a separate question.

From outline v0.5 the appraisal runs at two levels:

- **Level 1, the model's burden:** abductive adequacy against the mainstream model of the same data, scored on McCullagh's criteria (scope, power, plausibility, ad hocness, disconfirmation by accepted beliefs). Plausibility and ad hocness are scored relative to each framework's own background.
- **Level 2, accountability:** the programme's Lakatosian standing, unchanged. P-006, AD-002 and the auxiliary register continue to apply in full.

## Why this is recorded as an issue

Changing the yardstick right after a deficit appears is the move the Lakatosian criterion exists to catch. Two things distinguish this case, and a reader should be able to check both:

1. The clarification states what the work was for, and it comes from the author. It was not proposed to rescue a particular result.
2. Nothing at Level 2 is relaxed. The v0.4 Lakatosian scorecard stays visible in the outline next to the Level 1 scorecard, and the programme's registers are untouched.

## Result under Level 1

Parity on power, plausibility, ad hocness and disconfirmation. Short on scope by one datum class: shared human–ape ERVs (I-0009). The abductive case for closing that gap with A-007 is assessed in outline v0.5 §6F; Claude's verdict is that A-007 does not close it at acceptable cost. That verdict is open for JD's adjudication.

## Status

**Resolved 2026-11-15.** JD confirmed the two-level structure as the ERV sub-line's standing method (outline v0.5 §2A, §6D), and adopted the A-007 verdict with the risk accepted.
