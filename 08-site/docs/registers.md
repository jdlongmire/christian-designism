# Registers

The registers are the programme's enforcement instruments. A programme that specifies falsifiers in prose and tracks them nowhere has specified nothing.

## Predictions

The [predictions register](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/03-registry/PREDICTIONS.md) holds exactly one registered prediction:

- **P-006, ERV exogenization.** Registered in advance, with its scoring protocol, at [03-registry/preregistrations/P-006-e5-h2b-exogenization.md](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/03-registry/preregistrations/P-006-e5-h2b-exogenization.md). Resolution date: **2027-11-15**, when the parallel ERV programmes in this framework and in designed functional maturity are compared under a fixed protocol.

Two further predictions are held in draft, each gated on an open issue: P-002 waits on the hydrotectonics falsifier list (I-0001), and P-003 waits on the cosmology pre-registration (I-0003). Drafts are not registered, and the register says so.
