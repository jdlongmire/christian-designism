# Lines and Repositories

Programme work is carried in disciplinary lines, each with a repository of record, and in a small estate of related repositories. The index of record is [03-registry/REPOSITORIES.md](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/03-registry/REPOSITORIES.md), which lists all fifteen repositories by tier; this page summarizes the structure.

## Governing layer

- [christian-designism](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism): this programme's charter, hard core, registers, lines, and work packages.

## Homes and framework

- [biblical-worldmodel](https://github.com/jdlongmire/biblical-worldmodel): the programme home; manuscript, baselines, and the illustrated work.
- [designed-functional-maturity](https://github.com/jdlongmire/designed-functional-maturity): the functional maturity framework, and home of the parallel ERV programme behind the information firewall.

## Disciplinary programmes

- [erv-design-model](https://github.com/jdlongmire/erv-design-model): the primary ERV programme in this framework (epoch 1, special creation and functional maturity).
- [creation-cosmology-programmes](https://github.com/jdlongmire/creation-cosmology-programmes): the cosmology line's repository of record.
- [global-flood-hydrotectonic-model](https://github.com/jdlongmire/global-flood-hydrotectonic-model): the geology repository of record for the flood epoch.
- [post-flood-diversity](https://github.com/jdlongmire/post-flood-diversity): the post-flood diversification programme (epoch 5).

## Lines without a single home repository

The Adam, Eden, and Fall lines (epochs 2 and 3) work through the biblical-worldmodel manuscript and the systematics volumes rather than a dedicated repository. The mutational-load strand of the genetics line is an external literature line; the ERV sub-line's primary programme is erv-design-model, above.

## Presentation

The illustrated and apologetics work publishes from [oddxian-apologetics](https://github.com/jdlongmire/oddxian-apologetics) and the biblical-worldmodel site at [worldmodel.thinxai.net](https://worldmodel.thinxai.net/).
