# The Programme

Christian Designism is structured as a Lakatosian research programme: a hard core held immune by methodological decision, a protective belt of auxiliaries that can be revised under pressure, and a register of predictions and falsifiers that decides whether the programme is advancing or degenerating.

## The charter

[Consilience Is Not Consensus](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/01-charter/consilience-is-not-consensus.md) (v1.2) is the programme's charter. It sets the founding argument, the standards the programme accepts for itself, and the conditions under which its claims to standing lapse.

## The principles

The [principles statement](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/01-charter/christian-designism-principles.md) is the accepted foundational formulation: revelational and historical foundations, divine agency and natural processes, the five-epoch history model, and the programmatic consequences that follow.

## The hard core

The [hard core](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/02-programme/hard-core.md) carries eleven commitments in two parts.

Part I is epistemological:

- **HC-1.** Ontic realism: the world has a definite structure independent of our frameworks.
- **HC-2.** Historical reconstruction is framework-dependent: the same traces support different histories under different auxiliaries.
- **HC-3.** Parity of historical science: historical claims are judged by framework-level criteria, not by direct test alone.
- **HC-4.** Transcendental grounding: the preconditions of intelligibility are themselves part of what a framework must account for.
- **HC-5.** Scripture is authoritative regarding history, supplying boundary conditions on admissible reconstructions.

Part II is historical:

- **HC-6.** Creation was completed and pronounced good.
- **HC-7.** The initial state was functionally mature.
- **HC-8.** Adam is historical.
- **HC-9.** The Fall introduced death and corruption into the created order.
- **HC-10.** A global hydrological catastrophe reshaped the earth.
- **HC-11.** Humanity dispersed from a single post-flood population.

## The protective belt

Auxiliaries do the revisable work between the hard core and the data. The current belt, with verdicts, is kept in the [auxiliaries register](registers.md). One auxiliary, accelerated nuclear decay, has already been withdrawn as falsified; the belt is expected to lose members, and its losses are recorded, not hidden.
