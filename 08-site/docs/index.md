# Christian Designism

**A research programme governed by biblical historical claims, held to Lakatosian criteria.**

This site is the public front door of the programme. The documents of record live in the [governing repository](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism); this site presents the framework and points to them.

## The claim, and what it is not

The programme's founding argument is that the assertion "there is no scientific path to a young universe" conflates two distinct propositions: that the evidence, read inside the reigning framework, supports an old universe, and that no rival framework could read the same evidence differently. The charter, [Consilience Is Not Consensus](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/01-charter/consilience-is-not-consensus.md), argues that consilience is framework-relative: what counts as a converging line of evidence depends on the auxiliaries a framework brings to the data. Establishing that narrow point does not establish that this programme currently exhibits greater consilience than its rivals. It establishes that the contest is between frameworks, and must be judged as one.

## The history model

Designism holds to special and natural mechanisms within a history model of five epochs, each carrying an interpretive boundary:

1. **Special creation.** The good initial created order.
2. **Pre-fall.** The maturation of that created order during the pre-fall period.
3. **Post-fall.** The global corruption of the created order at the Fall.
4. **Global flood.** The impact of the worldwide flood on that corrupted order.
5. **Post-flood.** Ordinary processes and special interventions afterwards.

The full statement, with its mapping to the hard core, is the [principles document of record](https://github.com/jdlongmire/christian-designism/blob/main/01-charter/christian-designism-principles.md).

## Where the programme honestly stands

The registers are designed to be able to embarrass the programme, and they do. The programme has **no confirmed novel prediction yet**. Its one registered prediction, P-006 on ERV exogenization, resolves on 2027-11-15. Its standing falsifier F-002 is unmet. Its highest-value open question asks whether radiogenic daughter products fail the test of functional necessity (issue I-0006). Those facts are published here because the charter conditions the programme's standing on publishing them.
