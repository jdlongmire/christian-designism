# Hard Core

The commitments held immune from refutation by methodological decision, in Lakatos's sense. Everything else in the programme is protective belt and is recorded in [`registry/AUXILIARIES.md`](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md).

Stating the hard core separately and by number serves two purposes. It gives falsifier [F-001](../registry/FALSIFIERS.md#f-001--formal-contradiction-between-load-bearing-commitments) something specific to be tested against, since a contradiction must be derived from the commitments as the programme states them rather than from a critic's paraphrase. And it gives falsifier [F-004](../registry/FALSIFIERS.md#f-004--exegetical-failure-of-the-motivating-constraints) a specific target, since each historical commitment is annotated with the exegetical basis claimed for it.

Holding these immune is a methodological decision, not a claim that they are beyond question. The charter is explicit that this is what every mature research programme does, and that the presence of a protective belt is diagnostic of nothing by itself. What discriminates is whether adjustments to the belt generate novel testable content.

## Part I – Epistemological commitments

**HC-1. Ontic realism.** What exists is prior to what our methods are equipped to detect. Reductions of ontological questions to epistemic or procedural ones require independent justification.

**HC-2. Framework dependence of historical reconstruction.** Reconstruction of unrepeated past events requires auxiliary assumptions concerning initial conditions, system closure, rate constancy across the reconstructed interval, and the absence of singular intervening events. These are supplied by the governing framework and are not read off the data.

**HC-3. Epistemic parity of historical science.** Historical reconstruction is a legitimate inferential practice and is not methodologically inferior to experimental science. The operational and historical distinction is not deployed to grade the latter as second-class.

**HC-4. Transcendental grounding.** Every framework terminates in an ultimate authority it does not derive. The comparative question is whether that authority can account for the preconditions of the inquiry it authorizes: reliability of the senses, the uniformity grounding induction, the normativity of logical law.

**HC-5. Scripture as authoritative regarding history.** Scripture supplies historical information not recoverable by observation, functioning as boundary conditions on reconstruction rather than as gap-filling.

## Part II – Historical commitments

Each is a boundary condition on reconstruction, not a devotional theme. Each carries the exegetical basis claimed for it, on the inference standard of good and necessary consequence.

**HC-6. A creation completed and pronounced good.**
*Basis:* Genesis 1:31, 2:1-3.

**HC-7. A functionally mature initial state.**
*Basis:* The deployment language of Genesis 1 and 2, in which systems are described as operational at the point of their making rather than as initiated processes.
*Note:* This is the commitment from which auxiliary [A-001](../registry/AUXILIARIES.md#a-001--functional-maturity-of-origins) is developed, and it is the one on which falsifier F-002 bears.

**HC-8. A historical Adam.**
*Basis:* Genesis 2; the genealogical treatment in Luke 3; the Adam-Christ parallel in Romans 5 and 1 Corinthians 15, which requires the first term to be historical for the argument to hold.

**HC-9. A historical Fall introducing death into the human line.**
*Basis:* Genesis 3; Romans 5:12.

**HC-10. A global hydrological catastrophe.**
*Basis:* Genesis 6 through 9; the treatment in 2 Peter 3:5-7.

**HC-11. Subsequent dispersion.**
*Basis:* Genesis 11.

## What is not in the hard core

Recorded explicitly, because the most common critique of the programme is that anything can be moved into the immune zone when it comes under pressure.

The following are **protective belt** and are revisable on evidence. Each has a register entry with its current disposition:

- Functional maturity of origins as a geochronological *method* (A-001), as distinct from HC-7 which is the historical commitment it develops
- Any particular mechanism for the catastrophe of HC-10, including hydraulic collapse (A-003) and catastrophic plate tectonics (A-004)
- Any particular treatment of the light travel time problem, including the anisotropic synchrony convention (A-005)
- Accelerated nuclear decay (A-002), which is available to the programme and has been declined
- Any particular account of fossil succession, mutational load, or basin thermal history

A mechanism failing does not touch the hard core. HC-10 commits the programme to a global hydrological catastrophe, not to any account of how it proceeded. This is the ordinary structure Lakatos describes, and it is also why mechanism-level falsification is genuinely available: [P-002](../registry/PREDICTIONS.md#p-002--hydration-textures-required-by-global-flood-hydrotectonics) can fail without the programme failing, and the register is where that would be recorded.

The converse discipline matters more. Moving a commitment *into* the hard core after it comes under pressure is the manoeuvre the Lakatosian criterion exists to catch. Any addition to this file is a charter-level amendment, versioned, with the pressure that prompted it named.
